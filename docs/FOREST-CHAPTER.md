# Forest chapter — 0.11.0

The original Miracle Mine visual language and six clock learning configurations remain intact. Forest deliveries now form six authored days: first promises, next morning care, sprouts one week later, repairs after wind, flowers several weeks later, and a handover before Luca visits the harbor. Each day has three outbound parcels followed by three arrival records. Letters can be read before solving the records and reread from the map after completion.

Hour windows remain in stages 1–2. Stages 3–6 use exact hours, including 11 → 12 → 1, and stage 6 retains the hidden numeral challenge. Narrative afternoon labels do not introduce the later chapter's AM/PM mechanic. Existing save slots, stars, stage IDs, and six forest gift IDs remain compatible; old clears do not fabricate story completion.

The chapter ending explains the local desk handover and the separately awarded counter for the future central depot. Other chapters and endless retain their existing behavior.
